# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 10 October 2026**

This policy explains what the Hunt K Hash Cinematics app and its MCP servers ("the app", "we")
do with your information. The app lets an AI assistant that you choose read and manage your
Instagram and YouTube accounts on your instructions.

## 1. Information the app accesses

The app only accesses an account after you sign in and approve the permissions shown by Meta
or Google.

**Instagram (through the Instagram Graph API and Facebook Login)**

- Profile details of your Instagram business or creator account: username, name, biography,
  website, profile picture, follower and media counts
- Your posts, reels, stories and carousels, including captions, media links and timestamps
- Insights for your account and your media, such as reach, impressions, saves and views
- Comments on your media and their replies
- The list of Facebook Pages you manage, used only to find the linked Instagram account

**YouTube (through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API)**

- Your channel profile and statistics
- Your uploaded videos and their statistics
- Comments on videos
- Analytics for your channel, such as views, watch time and subscriber changes
- Public YouTube search results

**Sign-in tokens**

- Access tokens and refresh tokens issued by Meta and Google when you approve access

The app never sees or stores your Instagram, Facebook or Google password.

## 2. How the information is used

- To carry out the actions you ask for: reading your content and statistics, replying to
  comments, publishing media you provide, and uploading videos you provide
- To keep you signed in, by refreshing tokens

We do not use your information for advertising, do not sell it, and do not build profiles
from it.

## 3. Where the information goes

- **The AI assistant you connect.** Results of each request are returned to the MCP client you
  use (for example Claude). That provider handles the data under its own privacy policy.
- **Meta and Google.** Requests are sent to their APIs to read or change your account.
- **A file host you configure.** Instagram requires media to be fetched from a public URL. If
  you use the upload tool, the file you select is sent to the storage service you configured
  (Cloudinary or Amazon S3) and becomes reachable at a public link.

We do not share your information with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

## 4. Storage and retention

- Tokens are stored on the computer or server that runs the app, in a local file or database.
  They stay there until you sign out, delete them, or revoke access.
- The app does not keep a copy of your posts, comments, insights or analytics. They are
  fetched when requested and passed to your AI assistant.
- Edited video files are written only to the locations you choose on your own machine.

## 5. Security

Tokens are kept in files with restricted permissions where the operating system supports it.
Connections to Meta, Google and file hosts use HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is
perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Your choices

- **Revoke access at any time**
  - Facebook/Instagram: Settings → Apps and Websites → remove "Hunt K Hash Cinematics"
  - Google/YouTube: <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions> → remove the app
- **Delete your data:** see [User Data Deletion](data-deletion.md)
- **Ask a question or make a request:** open an issue at
  <https://github.com/Hash-Cine/Hunt-k-Hash-cinematics-privacy-policy-tc-license/issues>

## 7. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using its YouTube features you agree to be bound by the
[YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's handling of your data is
described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 8. Meta Platform data

Data received from Meta is used only to provide the features described above, in line with
the Meta Platform Terms. It is not transferred to data brokers or used for surveillance.

## 9. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly process their data.
